Our treatment process

We follow four key steps in providing our treatment services

01.

Initial assesment

We carry out initial assesment of the raw water quality in the client's facility either through a physicsl inspection or an online assesment depending on the client preference.

02.

Billing and payment

Based on assesment, a quote is sent to the client for approval. Once an agreement is reached, payment is made.

03.

Supply and Installation

After receiving initial payment, equipment are supply and install.

04.

Feedback

We receive feedbacks from client after installation to access customer satisfaction.
